Saida Lachgar is a Senior Frontend Developer with 6+ years of experience building production-grade web applications across e-commerce, enterprise monitoring, healthcare, and tourism industries.

Specializing in React, Symfony, and PrestaShop, Saida has a strong focus on performance optimization — Core Web Vitals, image delivery, and SEO — alongside interactive experiences built with Three.js and GSAP. Her work spans custom e-commerce storefronts, full-stack platforms, and everything in between.

Based in Agadir, Morocco, and educated at the Faculty of Science & Technology in Settat and the Specialized Institute of Applied Technology in Agadir, Saida is passionate about code that is fast, accessible, and maintainable.
